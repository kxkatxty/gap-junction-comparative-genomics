Finding genes in genomes

  • miniprot Coarse location of innexin/connexin-like loci on genome assemblies (discovery + curator search).
    Discovery (discover_innexins.py): -j 2 (insect/vertebrate splice model); score floor ≥ 30; HSP filters ≥ 60 bp, ≥ 20 aa query, ≥ 18% identity (short hits ≥ 35 aa and ≥ 22%); locus clustering gap ≤ 8 kb with ±2 kb flanks; large genomes (>4 GB) run per-contig with contig ≥ 1 Mb and up to 3 parallel jobs.
    Curator probe (stricter; preferred for presence calls): identity ≥ 0.20, length ≥ 280 aa, stop-free, Cys≥4 anywhere in the product (whole-protein count — not a 2+2 extracellular-loop motif test). Region locate (tblastn → per-region miniprot) is the default; whole-genome miniprot alone under-recovers on Dmel (3/8). Counts are floors; max values near the query-pack size may track queries rather than genome content.
    Why discovery “high confidence” was replaced: discovery acceptance used reference identity ≥ 15% (MIN_REFERENCE_IDENTITY = 15.0 in discover_innexins.py), which is too permissive for presence claims. Curator thresholds are the ones that survived manual locus checks.
  • samtools FAI indexing and region extraction for translating miniprot models.
    samtools faidx on genome FASTAs; region pull for CDS spans before Biopython translation. Translated products are often alignment spans without initiator Met — report as product/alignment length, not mature protein length.
  • MMseqs2 — candidate typing Protein search of candidate ORFs against trusted reference innexins/connexins.
    easy-search, --search-type 1 (protein), -e 1e-5, -s 5.7, format query,target,pident,qcov,bits. Discovery keep ranks used reference identity ≥ 15% (legacy; do not treat as high-confidence presence). Curator evidence is threshold-classified separately and should not share the same score column as rubric-computed discovery scores (hardcoded merge placeholders 55/40 were placeholders, not computed ranks).

Alignment, trees, similarity

  • MAFFT Multiple sequence alignment of phylogeny input FASTAs.
    Default mode mafft --auto (optional linsi / ginsi / fftns).
  • IQ-TREE 2 Phylogenetic trees for the innexin and connexin stories.
    -m MFP (ModelFinder Plus); -bb 1000 ultrafast bootstrap; -nt 2 threads (default in runner).
  • MMseqs2 — all-vs-all & clustering Within-family identity heatmaps and threshold sweeps (innexin / connexin similarity pages).
    Search: mmseqs search all-vs-all, -s 7.5, -e 1e-3, --max-seqs 1000 (innexin) / 800 (connexin).
    Cluster sweep: --min-seq-id innexin 0.20–0.70 (step 0.10); connexin 0.30–0.80; coverage -c 0.8, --cov-mode 0.
  • MMseqs2 — cross-family & discovery typing Innexin↔connexin homology check; discovery loci typed against reference connexins.
    Cross-family: easy-search innexin query → connexin target, -e 10, -s 7.5, --max-seqs 5. Homologous call = e-value ≤ 1e-3 and alignment length ≥ 80 aa (raw short fragments are not counted as homology).
    Discovery typing: easy-search, -e 1e-3, --max-seqs 5.
  • NumPy embeddings 3-mer composition + SVD and amino-acid composition cosines (family contrast pages).
    k-mers: k=3, vocabulary capped at top 800 kmers, truncated SVD to n_comp=20 (similarity pages) or 8 (direct innexin×connexin PCA); vectors L2-normalised; mean pairwise cosine sampled (seed 7, ≤ 8000 pairs).

Synteny and gene architecture

  • SynVoy Synteny-guided neighbourhood comparisons around selected innexin and connexin loci.
    Easy mode: --n_flanking_genes 5, --adaptive_max_regions 3, --enable_smith_waterman false, --exon_level_search false, --auto_params false, --multi_profile false, --max_genomes 3 (local queue). Reference insect runs also use --mmseqs_split_memory_limit 1G.
  • NCBI GFF microsynteny Exon counts, gene span, and cross-species neighbour sharing (GJA1 / GJA4 / innexin panels).
    Neighbour plots: ±180 kb around the gene of interest, ≤ 22 genes kept in the window. Neighbour conservation tiers from shared named genes across species (high = ≥3 shared in all species).
  • UniProt / Pfam Reference protein sequences and domain labels (e.g. Connexin PF00029).

Family sequence markers

  • Extracellular cysteine motif Conserved Cys pattern in the two extracellular loops (alignment filter).
    Innexin / pannexin: 2 Cys per loop → 4 per monomer.
    Connexin: 3 Cys per loop → 6 per monomer.
    Filters that require ≥4 Cys are a product-cleanliness check. Whole-protein Cys totals are often higher.
  • Hemichannel stoichiometry (literature) Not measured in this panel — labels used when reading papers.
    Innexon = octamer (8). Connexon = hexamer (6). Pannexon = heptamer (7; non-docking).
  • Coding-region introns / splicing From reference GFF gene models (exon_structures/).
    Connexin: ORF usually uninterrupted; models often ~2 exons (5′ UTR + coding exon). Panel median = 2.
    Innexin: coding-region introns common (panel median ~5 exons); one locus can yield several isoforms (e.g. shakB). GFF exon counts include UTR exons where annotated.
  • Annotation issues in the plots See also chapter 4 on the story path.
    Single-exon innexin medians (Ctenophora / non-insect Arthropoda): often TSA / ab initio ORFs without resolved introns.
    δ GJD “~22 exons”: GFF fusion of a compact connexin ORF with intron-rich flanks (DLGAP3 / SMIM12 near GJA4).
    Empty GJA1 flanks in X. laevis: short scaffold / assembly gap, not a gene island.
    Insect Inx2 (SF4) ≠ nematode inx-2 (SF3): same name, different clades — use tree-guided SF3 contrasts.
  • Early chordate bottleneck (literature) Welzel & Schuster 2022 (eLife) — gene-content framing, not a panel parameter.
    Lancelets lack connexins and keep one innexin with an extracellular N-X-S/T motif (pannexin-lineage marker). That bottleneck is the usual explanation for later connexin diversification. See pannexin path.
