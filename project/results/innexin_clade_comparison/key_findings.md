# Key findings

- Dataset integrates 30 reference proteins, 6 old-discovery loci and 289 curator-present loci across 28 species.
- Curator recoveries most often match unc-9 references (80/289 loci) — names like Inx2 in nematodes are best-hit labels, not proven orthologs.
- Outside insects, 154/286 kept loci map (via best-hit) to SF3 (Inx3/Inx7 + nematode-like references). This is a query-pack / best-hit composition effect, not independent agreement with the tree.
- Among discovery+curator loci, Arthropoda (non-insect) contributes the most (102 loci).
- Median miniprot product/alignment length (often no initiator Met): Rotifera 376 aa vs Mollusca 380 aa — not mature protein lengths.
- Median miniprot product/alignment length (often no initiator Met): Arthropoda (non-insect) 356 aa vs Rotifera 376 aa — not mature protein lengths.
- Median miniprot product/alignment length (often no initiator Met): Reference insect 372 aa vs Reference nematode 423 aa — not mature protein lengths.
- Reference DB covers classical labels: Inx1/ogre, Inx2, Inx3, Inx4/zpg, Inx5, Inx6, Inx7, other nematode, other/unknown, shakB, unc-7, unc-9.
- Note: flat median ~1 exon in Ctenophora / non-insect Arthropoda is often a TSA / ab initio gene model (continuous ORF as one exon), not intron-free innexins.
