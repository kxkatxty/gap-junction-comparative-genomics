gja4 (connexin) — microsynteny across 2 species

GFF microsynteny — annotated gene order around the gap-junction locus (no SynVoy).

Connexin paralogs such as cx38 / gjb3 / gjb4 / gja4 sit in a tight β-neighbourhood — typical of local tandem duplication (unequal crossing-over). Whole-genome duplications also put other paralogs on separate chromosomes (e.g. GJA1).
Comparing allotetraploid Xenopus laevis (L-homeolog) with diploid X. tropicalis: the flank kdm1a.L → tmem30b.L → zmpste24.L → smim12.L sits left of gja4.L in laevis, but is inverted and sits to the right of gja4 in tropicalis. Same neighbourhood; different orientation.
