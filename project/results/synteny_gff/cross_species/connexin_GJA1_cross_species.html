gja1 (connexin) — microsynteny across 2 species

GFF microsynteny — annotated gene order around the gap-junction locus (no SynVoy).

Empty flanks around Xenopus laevis gja1.L are not a biological “gene island”. In this plot the frog locus looks isolated while Danio rerio has a full neighbourhood. gja1.L sits on a short, poorly annotated scaffold (or near an assembly gap) in the X. laevis draft, so the GFF window has no reliable neighbours to draw.
